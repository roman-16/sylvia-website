02 / About

*2004

I’m Sylvia, an artist from Austria. I mostly paint with acrylic and oil, usually on canvas. Often realistic, sometimes abstract.

I don’t have a favourite style. I let all sorts of things inspire me: minimalist drawings that still go into full detail, and metallic art just as much. And if I had to name a favourite artist, it would be Van Gogh, because of Starry Night.

Most often I reach for green and grey. Not because I decided to. It just happens.

“When I paint I can switch my head off and find some quiet.”

03 / Commissions

Got a motif in your head, a photo, an idea? I paint on commission too.

Realistic is what I like best, but abstract or street style just as much. Acrylic or oil, on canvas, in the size that fits your wall.

How it works
Enquiry → agreeing on motif, format and price → progress updates while it takes shape → handover, signed, dated and sealed with glossing.
Time
A few weeks, depending on size.

On request. Depends on format and effort.
